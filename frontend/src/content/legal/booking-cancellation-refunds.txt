Users are encouraged to contact airlines directly for specific check-in luggage pricing.

Users undertake to pay all relevant statutory taxes, surcharges, and fees on the day of travel.

Infant & Child Fares: To qualify for infant prices, the child must be under 24 months old for the entire route (including both departure and return journeys). If the child turns 24 months or older during the return journey, a separate booking with a child fee is required. Infants and children must be accompanied by an adult in accordance with airline rules.

Amendment, Cancellation, and Refund Policy
Governing Terms: All tickets and bookings are subject to the terms and conditions of the respective airlines. By accepting the booking terms of fareclubs.com, you explicitly agree to the terms and conditions of the operating airline.

No-Show & Partial Modifications:

Refund requests for no-show or partially utilized flights must be submitted within 30 days of the travel date.

These specific refunds are subject to airline approval and may take up to 90 days to process.

Airlines do not allow partial cancellations for roundtrip fares or friends and family fares.

RNR Traveltech Private Limited applies a standard no-show fee of Rs. 400 per passenger per sector for domestic flights.

Infant Travel Restrictions: Most airlines do not allow infants younger than seven days to travel. Please contact the airline directly for specific policies.

Handling Fees: RNR Traveltech Private Limited will apply a reschedule/cancellation handling fee of Rs. 400 per passenger per sector (as detailed on your ticket copy), which is charged in addition to the airline's rescheduling or cancellation fees.

Policy Restrictions: Certain promotional or special fares may not be eligible for amendments or rescheduling based on airline rules. In case of cancellation, any promo discount amounts utilized will be forfeited and will not be processed for a refund.

Reissue & Rebooking Charges: All bookings are subject to airline-imposed reissue charges, varying by flight and booking class. If a booking is reissued, any applicable fare differences and reissue penalties will be charged. If the new fare is lower than the original, the difference will not be refunded, and standard rebooking fees will still apply.

Direct Airline Changes: Bookings can also be rescheduled directly via the respective airline’s call center. For rescheduling multi-itinerary reservations, you can reach out directly to our support number at 8069686968.

Refund Disbursement Process:

Refunds will only be initiated if explicitly claimed by the customer.

Approved refunds will be processed within 15–20 working days from the date of the request, subject to receiving the corresponding amount from the airline or vendor.

Refunds are processed strictly through the original mode of payment used during booking (e.g., if booked via a credit card, the amount will be credited back exclusively to that specific credit card).

The convenience fee paid at the time of booking is completely non-refundable.

The final refund will be executed after deducting the Company service/handling fee.

All cancellations made directly with the airline must be communicated back to us to initiate the refund process.
